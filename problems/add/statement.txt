Problem: add two integers

Read two integers on a single line, separated by whitespace, and print their sum
followed by a newline.

Input format : one line with two integers a and b, each in the range of a signed 32-bit int
               (-2147483648 .. 2147483647). Extra spaces or tabs around the numbers are allowed.
Output format: the mathematically correct sum a + b (it may need more than 32 bits).

Sample input : 7 5
Sample output: 12
